A white money deal refers to a transaction in which the entire sale consideration—every rupee—is paid out of income that is legally earned, fully disclosed, and properly accounted for, with all applicable taxes duly paid in accordance with the law.

The short-term “saving” on stamp duty and capital gain tax through under-registration often leads to significant long-term risks, including income tax notices, incorrect computation of capital gains at the time of future sale, reduced loan eligibility, and potential legal exposure. For honest salaried individuals, IT professionals, defense personnel, and NRIs with fully documented income—a white money transaction, in fact, safeguards and strengthens their financial profile.
